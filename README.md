## Remix IDE

This is a smart contract compiled and deployed using Remix IDE.

### Steps
- Oen browser and search for https://remix.ethereum.org/
- Click on the contracts folder and click "Create new file"
-Add your contract name and add .sol at the end.
- Write your smart contract in the file you have created.
- Click on Solidity compiler and compile your code.
- Click on Deploy. And on the new display choose "injected provider- metamask".
- Connect your wallet and click deploy.
